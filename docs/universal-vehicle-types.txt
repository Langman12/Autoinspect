1. The "Listening" Protocol (Audio)
Petrol Cars: Open the bonnet. Hold phone 30cm above the engine cover. Record 10 seconds of idle.
Diesel Trucks: kneel down by the front wheel arch (where the sound escapes the engine bay). Record the "clatter" from the side, not just the top.
Exhaust Test: Hold phone 1 meter behind the exhaust. Ask someone to rev to 2,500 RPM and let off.
What AI hears: It listens for the "crackle" on the way down (Burnt valves) or "blue smoke" sounds (misfires).
2. The "Panel Beater" Protocol (Visual)
The "45-Degree Scan": Do not take photos straight on. Take a video walking down the side of the car at a 45-degree angle.
Why: AI detects "ripples" in the reflection of the sky/surroundings that indicate body filler.
The "Fingernail Check": Take a close-up photo of the edge of the doors and the fuel cap.
Why: Painters often tape these off, leaving a hard "tape line" that the AI can see.
The "Under-Carriage" (Trucks/SUVs): Put the phone on the ground recording video and slide it under the rear bumper.
Why: Checks for bent chassis rails or fresh black "schutz" spray hiding rust.
