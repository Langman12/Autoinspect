Part 3: What the AI Looks For (The "Cheat Sheet")
To get the best results, ensure your video/audio captures these specific things so the AI can detect them:
For Mechanical (Audio):
The "Cold Start": Record the first 10 seconds of startup (this is when rattles are loudest).
The "Rev Drop": Rev the engine slightly (to 2500 RPM) and let it drop. Listen for a rattle on the way down (timing chain issues).
The "AC Load": Turn the AC on while recording; if the idle creates a clunking sound, it's a compressor or mount issue.
For Body (Visual):
The "Glance Down": Take a photo looking down the side of the car at a sharp angle. This highlights ripples in body filler (putty).
The "Bolt Check": Open the bonnet and take a clear photo of the bolts holding the fenders. If the paint on the bolts is cracked, the panel has been replaced/moved.
The "Tape Line": Take close-ups of the edges of the doors and fuel cap. Poor panel beaters often leave a hard paint line here.
