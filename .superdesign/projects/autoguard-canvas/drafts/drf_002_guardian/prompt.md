# Design Prompt: Guardian GPS (drf_002_guardian)

## Intent & Tension
Elevate Guardian GPS from a standard navigation screen into an elite automotive tactical radar system. It merges military flight-deck HUD aesthetics with hyper-localized Doppler weather telemetry and evasive route computing for extreme road hazards (hydroplaning, black ice, speed traps, debris).

## Preserved Core Realities
- Live GPS coordinate telemetry (latitude/longitude/altitude/heading)
- Live weather service integration (temperature, precipitation, wind gusts, atmospheric pressure)
- Simulated hazard radar blips (Potholes, Speed traps, Black ice, Thunderstorms)
- Co-pilot voice speech synthesis and spoken hazard advisories

## New Visual & UX Breakthroughs
- **Sweeping Circular Doppler Radar**: Continuous 360-degree rotating radar sweep with glowing distance rings, pulsing hazard blips, and interactive blip inspect tooltips.
- **HUD Speedometer & Threat Level Gauge**: High-contrast digital speed readout with speed limit ring indicator, G-force vector, and dynamic amber/red overspeed warning states.
- **Topographic Evasion Vector Map**: 3D wireframe terrain projection showing evasive route waypoints around severe storm cells.
- **Live Voice Co-Pilot Telemetry Terminal**: Monospaced military log showing streaming hazard alerts and navigational instructions.
