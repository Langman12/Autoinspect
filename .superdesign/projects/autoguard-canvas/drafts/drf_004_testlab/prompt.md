# Design Prompt: Test Lab (drf_004_testlab)

## Intent & Tension
Transform Test Lab from a diagnostic utility into a deep learning neural acoustic laboratory and benchmark playground. Engineers and auditors can generate synthetic mechanical fault audio, seed training sets, train acoustic classifiers in real time, and inspect high-resolution confusion matrix heatmaps and validation loss curves.

## Preserved Core Realities
- Local AI (Ollama) vs Cloud AI (Gemini) runtime health and latency benchmarking
- Synthetic acoustic audio generator (Rod knock, Lifter tick, Pulley whine, Clean idle)
- Auto-seeding training dataset (22-25 samples)
- Supervised acoustic classifier training loop with confusion matrix evaluation metrics
- Scientific research bibliography & citations

## New Visual & UX Breakthroughs
- **Interactive Synthesizer Control Deck**: Real-time frequency sliders, waveform selectors (Sine, Sawtooth, Square), and live playback audio synthesizer.
- **Glowing Confusion Matrix Heatmap**: Color-graded density matrix displaying Predicted vs Actual defect classes with accuracy metrics and misclassification highlights.
- **Neural Loss & Accuracy Convergence Curves**: Animated training loss curves depicting model optimization across epochs.
- **Service Latency & Uptime Radar**: Live indicator cards with millisecond latency ping readouts.
