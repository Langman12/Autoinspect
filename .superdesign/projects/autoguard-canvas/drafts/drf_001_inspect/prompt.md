# Design Prompt: Inspect Hub (drf_001_inspect)

## Intent & Tension
Transform the Inspect screen from a simple camera frame into a high-density, mission-critical tactical vehicle forensic laboratory. The user must feel like a senior automotive forensic auditor equipped with military-grade computer vision, acoustic spectrum analysis, and instantaneous VIN OCR verification.

## Preserved Core Realities
- 6-phase walkaround protocol (Front, Side, Engine, Undercarriage, Interior, Acoustic)
- Real-time Gemini Live AI / Ollama Local engine status
- Dynamic audio waveform & FFT harmonic peak detection
- Vehicle profile switching and instant report compilation

## New Visual & UX Breakthroughs
- **Tactical Cybernetic Reticle**: Dynamic crosshairs with corner telemetry, angle indicators, and live AI bounding box trackers.
- **Real-Time Acoustic Audio Visualizer**: Oscillating canvas with neon cyan/emerald spectral energy bars, harmonic frequency readouts, and simulated knock/tick alert tags.
- **Glassmorphic Vehicle Badge**: Holographic vehicle profile summary with quick-switch popover and VIN verification badge.
- **Live Multimodal Audit Feed**: Real-time streaming transcription log showing AI defect detections as they occur.
