# Design Prompt: Pathology Matrix (drf_003_matrix)

## Intent & Tension
Transform the static vehicle fault table into a forensic medical-grade diagnostic matrix. Every automotive defect (rod knock, spun bearing, Bondo concealment, battery cell thermal runaway, ADAS misalignment) is treated as a clinical pathology with distinct acoustic waveforms, chemical signatures, physical tolerances, and mandatory corrective protocols.

## Preserved Core Realities
- Complete diagnostic catalog across Acoustic, Exhaust Smoke, Undercarriage & Body, EV & Hybrid, and ADAS & Safety
- Severity grading: CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW
- Detailed root cause breakdown and GDVF action directives

## New Visual & UX Breakthroughs
- **Clinical Glassmorphic Dossier Cards**: High-contrast cards with glowing status indicators, frequency band pills, and failure action callouts.
- **Instant Reactive Search & Filter Chips**: Real-time filtering by category, severity level, or keyword (e.g. "200 Hz", "piston", "clamp", "ADAS").
- **Spectral Harmonic Preview**: Visual audio waveform bars for acoustic defects showing expected signature frequencies.
- **Fail-Safe Protocol Banners**: Color-coded banners for high-risk flags (e.g. "🔴 FAIL ENGINE — TOW TRUCK ONLY").
